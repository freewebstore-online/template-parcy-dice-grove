SOIL FIRST.

Bird of Paradise is a feeding plant. We mix in slow-release feed at repotting, top-dress every growing season, and water to the root, not the leaf. Feed the dirt and the blooms follow.

SUN MATH.

Full morning sun, filtered afternoon light. Six hours of direct sun builds flower buds; afternoon baking fries them. If your leaves bleach, count the hours your window actually gives.

SLOW IS FAST.

The honest truth: a young Strelitzia can take 3–5 years to flower. The trick is the spring dry rest — six weeks of drought sets the buds. Boredom, then bloom.
